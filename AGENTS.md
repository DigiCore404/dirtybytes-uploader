# Change records

Record every meaningful user-facing change in CHANGELOG.md before committing or releasing it. Include the version, date, resulting behavior and relevant limitations. Keep the complete history available in the Changelog menu and update instructions. Do not record trivial dash or punctuation-only label edits requested as cosmetic cleanup.

Preserve unrelated changes and keep this uploader separate from the other uploader and website repositories. Never commit credentials, runtime state, media, generated previews or upload history.
